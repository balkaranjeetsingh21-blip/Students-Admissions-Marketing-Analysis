# Students-Admissions-Marketing-Analysis
This Students Enrollment &amp; Marketing Analytics dashboard tracks key performance metrics. It breaks down revenue and lead sources across specific courses, counsellors, cities, and monthly trends to evaluate marketing and recruitment performance.
**Track Admissions | Analyze Performance | Grow Together**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed for **BG Techvista** to monitor student admissions, revenue generation, counsellor efficiency, and marketing channel performance from **April 2025 to March 2026**.

---

## Project Overview

This dashboard provides a centralized view of the student recruitment funnel—from initial marketing leads and campus visits to final course enrollments. It enables stakeholders to identify top-performing courses, evaluate regional reach, and track month-over-month growth.

## Dashboard Sections & Visuals

### 1. Interactive Slicers (Left Navigation Panel)
Users can dynamically filter the entire report by:
* **Admission Date**
* **Course**
* **Region**
* **Counsellor**
* **Lead Source**

### 2. Trends & Funnel Analysis
* **Monthly Revenue & Admission Trend:** A dual-axis line chart comparing revenue against enrolled student counts across the academic year.
* **Monthly Visits vs. Admissions:** A clustered column chart tracking monthly footfall against final enrollments from April 2025 to March 2026.
* **Lead Conversion Funnel:** Visualizes drop-off across stages (**480 Total Leads** → **480 Total Visits** → **458 Enrolled Students**).

### 3. Course & Marketing Performance
* **Sales by Course:** Horizontal bar chart ranking courses by revenue, led by **Civil (₹10,42,000)**, **Mechanical (₹8,34,000)**, and **Coding/Programming (₹7,40,000)**.
* **Top 5 Courses by Visits:** Table highlighting visit volume and conversion efficiency across top programs.
* **Lead Source Breakdown:** Donut chart categorizing the 480 total visits across **Organic Search**, **Social Media**, **Referral**, **Email Marketing**, and **Direct Inquiry**.

### 4. Team & Regional Insights
* **Counsellor Performance:** Tracks individual conversion metrics for **Amandeep Kaur (94.50%)** and **Sukhjeet Kaur (96.18%)**.
* **Top Cities by Enrolled Student:** Column chart highlighting student intake across key Punjab regions, including **Barnala (32)**, **Amritsar (30)**, **Fazilka (29)**, **Bathinda (28)**, and **Tarn Taran (28)**.

## Tools & Technologies Used

* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Data Modeling & Calculations:** DAX (Date/Time intelligence, Month-over-Month variance, Conversion Rate formulas)
* **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Power Query

---

## How to Use

1. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.
2. Use the **date range selector** in the top right (`01-04-2025` to `31-03-2026`) or the **left-hand filter pane** to slice data by specific courses, regions, or counsellors.
3. Hover over individual charts and data points to view detailed tooltips.
